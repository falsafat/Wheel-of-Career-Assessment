# Wheel of Career Assessment

This assessment helps generate a *Wheel of Career* score across 8 career dimensions.

Each section includes 5 multiple-choice questions.  
Each answer is scored from 1 to 5.  
Each section score is then converted into a 10-point wheel score.

---

## Scoring Method

| Answer | Score |
|---|---:|
| A | 1 |
| B | 2 |
| C | 3 |
| D | 4 |
| E | 5 |

### Section Score Formula

For each section:

```text
Section Score = Total points for the 5 questions ÷ 25 × 10
```

### Example

If someone scores 18 out of 25 in Career Growth:

```text
18 ÷ 25 × 10 = 7.2
```

The wheel score for Career Growth is:

```text
7.2 out of 10
```
